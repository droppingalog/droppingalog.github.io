EP. 000 — THE PILOT

Two buddies. One log.

Dropping A Log started as a group chat that got out of hand and turned into a microphone. Every week we sit down, hit record, and talk through whatever's stuck in our heads — bad decisions, good stories, and the stuff nobody else wants to log for the record.

No script. No filter. Just two guys keeping an honest log of everything that happened since the last one.
